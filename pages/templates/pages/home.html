How it decides

  1. 1

    Forecast a range

    A model trained on past sales, promotions, bank holidays and the weather gives a likely range for each product, not one number.

  2. 2

    Weigh waste against empty shelves

    The margin you lose on an empty shelf and the cost of a binned item set where in that range to order. You choose how much an empty shelf matters.

  3. 3

    Explain each line

    Each order shows the two things that moved its forecast most, such as a promotion or the day of the week, so you can overrule it when you know better.

On 44% of store-product days in the data, a product sold out at some point, so sales records understate what customers wanted. That's why you can choose how much an empty shelf matters.
