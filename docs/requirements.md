# Requirements

## Who it's for

The main user is the owner or manager of an independent grocery shop who places a fresh-food order each day for the next day's delivery. Later, a wholesaler supplying many such shops could see combined waste trends from the shops that opt in.

## User stories

1. As a shop owner, I want tomorrow's order for each fresh product in one short list, so I can place the order in a few minutes.
2. As a shop owner, I want to see the range of sales to expect, not only one number, so I know how sure the recommendation is.
3. As a shop owner, I want to choose whether to lean towards fewer leftovers or fuller shelves, because I know my customers and my margins.
4. As a shop owner, I want a plain-English reason for each line, so I can tell when the recommendation is missing something I know, such as a local event.
5. As a shop owner, I want to change any quantity before I order, because I'm responsible for the order.
6. As a shop owner, I want the recommendations to account for promotions, bank holidays and the weather.
7. As a shop owner, I want to see how much waste the tool would have saved, before I trust it.

Phase 1 covers stories 1 to 6. The override field (story 5) isn't saved anywhere yet, and story 7 is answered by the benchmark on the results page rather than for the owner's own shop.

## Phase 2 stories

8. As a shop owner, I want to upload my own sales history as a CSV and get clear errors if it's wrong.
9. As a shop owner, I want the order emailed to me each morning.
10. As a shop owner, I want to export or delete my shop's data.
11. As a wholesaler, I want combined, anonymised waste trends from shops that have agreed to share them, and each shop must be able to withdraw that consent.

## Notes from shop managers

I haven't interviewed shop managers yet. I plan to show the order page to managers of local independent shops and record what they say here, including what they do today to decide quantities.
